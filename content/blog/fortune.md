I cracked open a fortune cookie last night, after a full meal, unsure what I was hoping for - was I looking for validation in my hopes for the future, or for something new and exciting to look forward to?

Even if you don’t believe in fortune cookie success, you can’t deny the rush they inspire.

But when that cookie came apart, there was no fortune within - it was as empty as my expression. Somewhere between surprise and dismay, I sat there wondering what it meant. Trust me to read too much into something with nothing written on it at all.
