I'm not very good with too much change and uncertainty, and it can seem like they have become a part of our day-to-day. I like to have more answers than questions, but sometimes no one has the answers you're looking for. While it is good to look for rainbows when it rains and hang on to the hope that things will find a way to work themselves out, I can't help but wonder: where do we go from here?
