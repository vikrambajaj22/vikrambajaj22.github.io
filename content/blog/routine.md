We complain about the monotony but cling to routine for it is our safety net, letting us glide on autopilot. For when change feels overwhelming, we seek comfort in familiarity and predictability. Doing one thing differently is like doing everything differently. But is a life without unknowns really better than a life full of unanswered what-ifs?
